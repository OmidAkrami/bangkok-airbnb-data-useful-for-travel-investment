All districts

Click a column to sort. Price and occupancy are active entire homes with a 1 to 6 night minimum. Share of revenue covers all active listings.

Occupancy uses Inside Airbnb's review-based model: reviews in the last 12 months, divided by a 50% review rate, times the longer of 3 nights or the minimum stay, capped at 70% of the year. Good for ranking, rough as a level.

Data quality. Feb, Mar and Apr 2026 were partial scrapes and there is no May 2026 snapshot. From Feb 2026 most dormant listings drop out of the scrape, so trends use active listings.

Steps in occupancy. Modelled occupancy moves in steps. For a 1 to 3 night minimum one review is 6 nights, 1.6 points of the year, so several districts share a median like 16.4% or 23.0%.

Price timing. From March 2026 the price is a quote for a stay a few days after the scrape, so these are late-September prices, the low season.

Source. Inside Airbnb, Bangkok, CC BY 4.0. Prices are asking prices before fees, not what guests paid. Listing locations are shifted by up to about 150 m, fine at district level.
